A concept film cut into three stripes and released as three, so the campaign arrives the way the mark does: one line at a time.

Year
2026
Kind
Image
Role
Concept, art direction, generative production

9:16 reel, three-part release

The format problem

Three stripes, three drops.

This is not one film chopped into parts. Each stripe is a complete night on the same street, and the three only resolve into a campaign once they have all been posted.

Structure

Stripes.

Numbered like the mark itself, and released out of order on purpose.

  1. Stripe 1

    Walk

    A point-of-view ending on an empty street, shot to feel carried rather than composed.

  2. Stripe 2

    Street

    The city closes in. Neon, parked cars, and nothing else moving.

  3. Stripe 3

    Turn

    The last shoe you will see, and the only moment the face is given.

How it was built

Pipeline.

Boarded in Figma, generated through Higgsfield for continuity, then held to one camera position so the location reads as real.

Board

Three-stripe structure and shot order fixed on one Figma board before generation.

Generate

Higgsfield carrying character and wardrobe continuity across all three nights.

Compose

One camera position held across the set, so the street reads as a place rather than a backdrop.

Export

Vertical masters cut for feed and released as separate posts rather than one film.
