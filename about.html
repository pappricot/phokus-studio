Method

A controlled theatre for software and images.

Phokus is built around a simple studio law: the product has to function, the image has to be directed, and the system behind both has to survive revision. Mobile screens, landing pages, AI campaigns, video tests, and internal tools all need the same kind of disciplined taste.

The work moves between React Native architecture, Supabase-backed product flows, deployment pipelines, prompt structures, node-based AI workflows, and production plans for social-first visuals.

The result is part portfolio, part human dossier, part command surface. It is meant to feel ceremonial on arrival and practical once the brief becomes real.
