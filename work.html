Case 04

Campaign Worlds

Campaign systems for fashion, wellness, cinematic storytelling, and social-first production. Most are generated; one is not, and each says which on its own page. Filmed rather than generated: Alessandro Taini, a portrait of the concept artist’s body of work shot on a camera, cut on a timeline, and screened at Comic-Con San Diego 2026.
