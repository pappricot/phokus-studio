Image / fashion campaign

Real Metal

An AI-generated fashion campaign made for the Runway AI Film Festival, asking whether authenticity survives an artificial system — and answering in the language of a security camera.

Year
2026
Kind
Image
Role
Concept, art direction, generative production
Frames
9:16 reel, CCTV-framed stills

The format problem

Shot as evidence, not as advertising.

A jewellery campaign usually shows metal in a lightbox. Here every frame is presented as a recovered camera feed, so the hardware reads as something caught rather than something styled.

Structure

Real metal, real attitude.

The campaign is built on one question and keeps returning to it rather than resolving it.

  1. Frame I

    The checkpoint

    A gate that scans for metal, standing in a world built to sell it.

  2. Frame II

    The subjects

    Faces that know they are being recorded and hold the lens anyway.

  3. Frame III

    The verdict

    The question is not whether the metal is real, but whether the attitude is.

How it was built

Pipeline.

Generated end to end, then graded and framed so the artificial origin becomes part of the argument instead of something to hide.

Board

Campaign concept and shot order fixed before a single frame was generated.

Generate

Character, wardrobe, and set continuity carried across every camera position.

Grade

A desaturated concrete palette, so the red sign and the chrome are the only colour left.

Frame

The CCTV overlay applied last: camera number, timestamp, recording state.
